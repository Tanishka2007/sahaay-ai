What is Sahaay: Universal Triage Bridge?

              
Sahaay is an autonomous emergency automation engine powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. It ingests messy voice transcripts, disaster reports, and physical sensor telemetry to produce verified civilian directives and structured CAD/FHIR dispatch manifests.

            

            

              
What is the Predictive Ripple Effect Engine?

              
Whenever an emergency is decoded, Sahaay predicts 2nd and 3rd order impacts (e.g. traffic blockage delaying ambulances to City Hospital, cascading hospital ICU bed saturation) and autonomously executes mitigations before bottlenecks occur.

            

            

              
How does Client Profile Injection work?

              
Your saved profile (blood group, chronic conditions, and lethal allergies like Penicillin) is automatically attached to backend triage payloads so the AI cross-verifies medication contraindications before first responders arrive.

            

            

              
What is Conflict Detection?

              
When citizen panic contradicts physical reality (e.g. viral rumors claiming a dam collapsed while SCADA sensors report closed radial gates), the engine flags a discrepancy and enforces a fail-closed safety priority.
